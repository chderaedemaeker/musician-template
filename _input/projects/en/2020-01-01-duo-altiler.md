Duo Altiler is a Belgian violin-piano duo formed by Veronique De Raedemaeker and Alexander Declercq. Their repertoire spans from Classical to Contemporary, featuring works by Beethoven, Bartók, Debussy, Ravel, Schnittke, and Lutosławski. In 2025, Duo Altiler reached the finals of the Supernova competition at De Singel, Antwerp.
