Trio Gilu is a unique ensemble combining soprano, violin and harp. The trio has performed extensively across Belgium and released the CD 'Garland — Songs for Soprano, Violin and Harp' on Etcetera Records in 2022. Their repertoire includes works by Britten, Haydn, Rimsky-Korsakov, as well as premieres of new compositions by De Gheselle, Letelier and Cánovas i Parés.
