ÉRMA Ensemble is an international collaborative project based in Cologne, Germany, consisting of a specialized group of soloists and chamber musicians performing contemporary music. Supported by Deutscher Musikrat, the ensemble focuses on the performance of new works and has appeared at the Acht Brücken Festival Cologne.
