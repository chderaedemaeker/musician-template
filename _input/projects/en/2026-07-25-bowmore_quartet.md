Bowmore approaches contemporary music as part of a living tradition: every work was new once, and every score carries traces of its time, place and people. From Belgian voices and late twentieth-century landmarks to world premieres and forgotten gems, the quartet brings this music into direct contact with listeners — especially when it refuses to behave.
