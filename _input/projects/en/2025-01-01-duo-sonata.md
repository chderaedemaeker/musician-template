A long-term collaboration exploring the complete violin-piano sonata repertoire of Beethoven. Over the course of two seasons, all ten sonatas are performed in a series of recitals across Belgium and the Netherlands.

This project aims to bring these cornerstone works to new audiences in intimate venues.
