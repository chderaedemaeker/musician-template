Since my childhood, I have been drawn to finding meaning in silence, and I believe strongly that as a listener, we can grow closer to each other while listening to the silence in between sound. It is probably still just the beginning of a long way to go, but I‘m curious to see how sharing the steps can motivate me to keep looking for the meaning in it.
